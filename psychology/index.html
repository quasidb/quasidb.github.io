The tools tell you where to trade. This section is about the part no tool can do for you: sticking to the plan once you're in. It's written by a trader who still finds this hard, not by someone pretending to have it solved.

The one rule

Never trade sentiment on its own. Use it only together with technical and fundamental analysis, and with strict risk management. Good psychology starts with a plan you can trust.
