The idea

Most retail traders end up on the wrong side. The sheet shows you which side that is.

Every number in the QDB Sheet is read contrarian. When the crowd piles in one direction, that's colour-coded as the opposing trade — so red always means "the crowd is long", and green always means "the crowd is short".

The QDB Score

Not "is it trending?" — "is the crowd on the wrong side of a trend?"

Plenty of tools tell you price is above or below a level. The QDB Score only lights up green when price is confirming a direction and retail traders are leaning hard the other way.

  • Pivot checks — monthly, weekly and daily pivots all agree on direction.
  • Open checks — price is on the right side of today's and this week's open.
  • Sentiment shift — the crowd is moving further into the losing side, not out of it.
  • Score change — see which pairs are building conviction through the day.

FAQ

Questions people ask before joining

What do I actually get with Premium?

Full Discord access plus the QDB Sheet itself (a Google Sheet), shared to the Gmail address you give in your PayPal note. Until 31 December the Basic plan includes all of this for $20.

Is this a signal service?

No. The sheet gives you context — where the crowd is positioned and whether price is confirming against them. You decide whether, when and how much to trade.

Where does the sentiment data come from?

Publicly available retail positioning data from multiple brokers and sentiment sources, collected and processed throughout the day, plus FXCM's Speculative Sentiment Index.

Do I need to be technical to use it?

Not at all. If you can open a Google Sheet you can use it. The colours do most of the talking: green means the crowd is short, red means the crowd is long.

Which markets are covered?

The major and minor FX pairs, plus indices such as US500, US30, GER40, UK100 and FRA40, and commodities including gold and oil.

Do I need a Gmail address?

Only for the QDB Sheet, which is a Google Sheet shared to your Google account. Discord access needs just your Discord username.

Can I cancel?

There's nothing to cancel. Each PayPal payment covers one month. If you don't renew, your access simply ends when your paid month runs out.
