Quick questions

Is this a signal service?

No. The tools give you context: where the crowd is positioned and whether price agrees. Use sentiment alongside your technical and fundamental analysis and strict risk management, never on its own.

Do I need a Gmail address?

Only for the QDB Sheet, which is shared to a Google account. Discord access needs just your Discord username.

PayPal or Patreon?

PayPal is pay as you go: each payment covers one month (or a year with the Christmas deal) and nothing renews by itself. Patreon renews monthly until you stop it in your Patreon account, so it suits people who want to set it and forget it.

Can I try the QDB Sheet first?

Yes. Start a 14-day free trial with your Google email. No card, and access simply ends after two weeks unless you choose a plan.

Can I ask before I buy?

Of course. Use the contact form or message @quasi_db on Telegram.
