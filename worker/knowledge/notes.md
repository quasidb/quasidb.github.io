## Core teaching points
- Retail sentiment is read contrarian: a crowd that is mostly long is read as bearish, mostly short as bullish.
- Sentiment alone is not a reason to trade. Only act at strong daily or weekly supply and demand levels.
- "Only trade the SLCs at key strong technical levels." Never place a stop at the obvious swing high or low, or inside the crowd's stop loss cluster.
- Market makers do not necessarily go for the larger stop loss cluster first; price often takes the smaller, nearer one.
- QDB is a decision aid, not a signal service.
