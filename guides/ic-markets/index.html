IC MARKETS OR ANOTHER BROKER?

You don't have to use IC Markets to follow the Trade Mirror.

But with IC Markets you mirror Dave's setups exactly, because you trade with the same broker as Dave. The only lag is a short delay, usually milliseconds, from the FX Blue connection. With another broker, prices, spreads and symbols can differ, so your results can too.

Before you switch it on

Automated and copied trades carry the same risk as trades you place yourself, plus their own: software bugs, lost connections, delays and slippage can all cause losses or missed exits. Start small, watch it closely, and keep sentiment paired with strong technical levels. You decide whether to follow, and how much to risk.

Questions

I only have a demo account. Which path am I?

Choose "I already have an IC Markets account". Open a live account in the same client area, then carry on from funding. Demo accounts can't be linked.

Do I fund first or chat first?

Either. Funding the live account and asking support to merge it can happen in any order. You need both done before the email confirmation counts.

Why do I have to email support as well as chat?

The chat logs the request. The email makes it formal and binding on IC's side, and their reply is your proof that the merge is done.

What if support doesn't understand the request?

Paste the exact message from step "Ask support to merge" and quote Campaign ID 55485. If you're still stuck, message Dave on Telegram at @quasi_db and he'll help.

Do I need IC Markets to use the sentiment EAs?

No. Anyone with an FX Blue-connected account can follow the Trade Mirror. With IC Markets you mirror Dave's setups exactly, with only a short connection delay. With another broker, prices and spreads can differ.

Are the EAs finished?

Not yet. They're in a test phase and will keep changing. Your test results help shape them, so please share what you see.
