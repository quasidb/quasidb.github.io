What's new on QDB this November

Monday 23 November 2026 · 2 minute read

A lot has been added to the site this month. Rather than shout about each thing on the homepage, here it all is in one place. Pick what's useful and ignore the rest.

Free every day

  • A new address: the site now lives at quasidb.com.
  • Retail sentiment for 28 pairs, updated each weekday after the European close, with the crowd's positioning and the weekly COT read for each pair.
  • COT report this week: FX, gold and silver positioning, rebuilt every Friday.
  • Beat the Crowd, a daily puzzle built from that day's real crowd data.
  • Free downloads: a free version of the QDB Sheet, plus free MT5 indicators.

New guides

  • Sentiment: the final layer of any trading system. Our answer to "how do I use your tool in a trade?"
  • Stop loss clusters, the Order Book and the crowd train, each with step-through animations.
  • Get QDB free with IC Markets, step by step.
  • Every QDB Sheet tab video, now on the Guides page.

Games

  • What kind of trader are you? A 3-minute quiz. Nothing you answer is stored.
  • The Trader's Advent Calendar: 24 days of trading challenges through December.
  • The rest are on the Games page.

And now, this blog

Two kinds of post will appear here: short site updates like this one, and market notes on news that moves currency sentiment, like the UK–EU reset and the pound. Market notes always pair the news with levels and risk, because sentiment is never a trade on its own. Follow along with the RSS feed.
